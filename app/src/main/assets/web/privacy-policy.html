Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the application Memory Slideshow & Dynamic TV Frame (“we”, “our”, or “the Application”) collects, uses, maintains, and protects user information.

1. Information Collection and Storage

a) User Photos, Music, and Media Files:

  • All photos, images, and audio tracks imported or selected by you are processed and stored strictly locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and IndexedDB.
  • We do NOT transmit, host, copy, or store your personal photos or media on any external servers. Your media files never leave your device.

b) Technical Device Information:

  • The Application may read basic technical information such as screen aspect ratio, device orientation, and operating system language in order to render full-screen 16:9 displays properly on Android TV, Fire TV, and tablets.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

a) Start.io Ad Network:

The Application includes non-intrusive advertisements provided by Start.io (App ID: 210307679). To serve relevant ads and maintain security, Start.io may automatically collect and process technical data, such as:

  • Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, Amazon Fire Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse regional location
  • Device model and ad performance metrics

To learn more about Start.io's privacy practices, please visit the Start.io Privacy Policy.

b) Online Audio & Radio Streaming:

The Application allows users to play background audio through standard public HTTP/HTTPS online radio and audio streams (such as Swiss Classic Radio, lofi ambient streams, or user-provided streaming URLs). Audio streams are fetched directly via standard HTML5 audio elements. No personal credentials or profiles are sent to external streaming endpoints.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Required to load remote advertising banners (Start.io) and play online audio streams.
  • WAKE_LOCK / Keep Screen On: Keeps the TV or tablet display active during slideshow playback.
  • Media Selection: Used solely to allow you to choose local photos and audio tracks on your device.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Data Security & Control

Because your personal files are stored locally, you maintain complete control. You can delete individual items or clear all data at any time via the Application's settings menu or your device's application manager.
